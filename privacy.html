HH Health privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

HH Health is a private, personal health app built for and used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public, and new sign ups are switched off.

What data the app uses

The owner enters their own health information, such as measurements, food, medicines and check ins. With the owner's permission, the app also reads fitness and sleep data from their own Google account through the Google Health API, read only: activity, heart and body measurements, sleep, and irregular rhythm notifications.

Where it is kept

All data is stored in the owner's own private database, protected by sign in and row level security. Access tokens for Google are stored encrypted on the server and are never sent to the app or a browser.

How Google user data is used

Data from Google is used only to show the owner their own health information and trends inside the app. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with anyone else, and never used to train AI models. The app only reads this data; it never writes to or changes the owner's Google account.

HH Health's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI features

Some optional features send the owner's own text or photos to an AI provider the owner has chosen, only after the owner turns that feature on. Data from Google Health is not sent to AI providers unless the owner explicitly turns on a feature that needs it.

Removing access and deleting data

The owner can disconnect Google Health in the app at any time, which deletes the stored Google tokens, and can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The owner can delete any data in the app, or the whole database.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign in screen.
